# IU Work Tracker — Product Vision

## Purpose

IU Work Tracker is a personal operating system for an Intermediate Unit STEM and district-support professional. It reduces documentation burden by capturing an activity once and reusing that record for reflection, planning, district engagement, project history, impact evidence, leadership summaries, and future ORBIT reporting.

The primary user is the professional doing and documenting the work. Secondary future audiences include IU leadership and reporting partners, but the application must remain optimized for fast, accurate entry by the primary user.

## Product philosophy

**Log it once. Use it everywhere.** A canonical Work Record is the source for every view and report. Projects, organizations, contacts, categories, evidence, follow-ups, and ORBIT classifications add context without creating parallel activity databases.

The interface uses a kiosk or mission-control model: a finite command center, large task-oriented actions, compact status, focused workspaces, and progressive disclosure. One screen should map to one mental task. Quick entry keeps optional reporting details out of the way until relevant.

## IU Work Tracker versus ORBIT

ORBIT is one reporting destination and taxonomy. IU Work Tracker covers the user's whole portfolio, including work outside STEM and the Statement of Work. A record can be marked ORBIT-reportable and mapped to one primary and multiple supporting deliverables. ORBIT data is therefore a reporting facet of normal work—not the application's root structure.

## Product principles

- Make logging work the clearest and fastest action.
- Store precise duration; derive reporting days and quarters from configuration.
- Distinguish activity, output, outcome, evidence, and next step.
- Reuse canonical projects, organizations, people, and categories.
- Preserve drafts and explain synchronization failures.
- Use stable application IDs independent of storage-provider IDs.
- Treat SharePoint as the intended institutional source of truth.
- Build accessible, keyboard-friendly, touch-friendly screens from the start.

## V1 success

V1 proves the universal record and the daily workflow: the user can orient quickly, log routine work in about a minute, review today and history, edit records, connect work to projects and organizations, optionally attach STEM/ORBIT classification, and see honest summaries derived only from labeled development sample data or their own entries.
